Luna 0.0.3

  • Her coat is now a 512x256 sheet (8x the vanilla cat texture): fur strands, darker agouti banding, a pale ruff band and a light fringe along every edge so her outline reads fluffy.
  • Whisker lines are painted radiating from her pink nose; the whiskers sit on the game's own muzzle pixels so they line up with her face.
  • Her eyes went back to the size and position the game uses for cat eyes (recoloured chartreuse). An earlier pass enlarged them on the sheet and the extra pixels landed on other parts of her head, so her face looked wrong in game; that pass is reverted.
  • The mod icon is now pixel art of her face in her coat colours instead of a photo crop.
  • Manifest tidy-up: the example mod's placeholder links (fabricmc.net, fabric-example-mod) and a stray key were removed and a date added, so the archive's own metadata says what this mod actually is.
  • In the icon, the two light bars outside the tips of her ears were dropped: her ears now end against the empty background, nothing else about her face, her whiskers or her ruff moved.
  • The packaged file is now named luna-fabric-1.20.1-0.0.3.jar, the way the other Homestead jars are named (mod id, loader, game version, mod version), so the version and target are readable off the file itself; nothing inside the jar changed because of the rename.
  • Nothing about her behaviour, her spawn egg or her name changed in this version.

Luna 0.0.2
